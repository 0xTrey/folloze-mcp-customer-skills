# One-to-One Account Experience Acceptance

Use this checklist for a custom MCP/HTML named-account experience before Folloze save, delivery, or publish.

## Identity And Header

- The seller is the primary visual brand.
- When co-branding is required, the seller and target logos appear together in the top header.
- The target logo is an official wordmark or approved mark with a named source and working asset URL.
- Both logos remain legible and proportionate at desktop, 390px, and 320px.
- A target logo that appears only in the hero or a lower card fails this gate.

## Account Argument

- The parent target account owns the headline, primary CTA, and leadership outcome.
- Supporting business units appear only where they strengthen a sourced point.
- The first viewport answers why now, what the seller does, what changes for the target, and what leadership
  should do next.
- Replacing the target with a peer would require rewriting the hook, mechanism, outcome, and CTA.
- Every material claim is public, approved, or clearly framed as a scenario.

## Interaction

- The page contains one primary decision-advancing interaction.
- The interaction answers a named buying question and produces a useful result or path.
- A calculator uses approved inputs, formulas, assumptions, and ownership. If those do not exist, use a
  diagnostic, scenario model, readiness assessment, maturity score, comparison, or role path instead.
- The interaction works with keyboard input and on desktop, 390px, and 320px.

## Composition And Brand

- Headline capitalization and terminal punctuation match the harvested brand evidence.
- Button treatment uses the observed component family and the effective rendered label style.
- Full-width section bands are used when the source design establishes that rhythm.
- At least three composition types are used when the content supports them.
- Two adjacent equal-card grids are not allowed unless the source design clearly requires them.
- No small kicker, large headline, and explanatory subtitle are stacked as the default section introduction.

## State Receipts

Report local source, rendered QA, Folloze save, Folloze readback, draft preview, publish, and anonymous
verification separately. None of these states implies another.
