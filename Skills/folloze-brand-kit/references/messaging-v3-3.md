# Folloze Messaging V3.3

Reviewed 2026-10-27 against the approved October 26, 2026 update. This public-safe alignment supersedes earlier messaging in this bundle.

## Lead Positioning

Lead with AI-driven ABM and define it in the same piece: your AI researches and writes; Folloze turns that work into campaigns for accounts across the funnel, with a governed experience that adapts by account, persona, and stage and captures engagement to guide the next move.

Promise one-to-one impact at segment-level effort. Use "feels one-to-one" for impact. One-to-one can also describe a single named-account build, such as a deal room.

Do not promise "1:1 for every account," "1:1 at scale," "one-to-one at scale," "unlimited," or "without limits." Do not claim AI builds the campaign on its own.

Microsite names an output. Describe Folloze as delivering campaigns and experiences, not as a landing page builder. Avoid headless and open architecture externally; name what the workflow does.

## Capabilities And Workflow

Build. Activate. Signal. remains the capability display register.

Research, Deploy, Learn describes the marketer's workflow:
1. The customer's AI and approved sources provide research and writing.
2. Folloze turns that input into a live, governed account experience.
3. Engagement shows who interacted, with what, for how long, and what should inform the next move.

Include engagement signal whenever explaining how it works. Lead with the outcome: know which accounts are leaning in, what they read, and what to send next. Supported integrations route engagement into sales and marketing systems. Do not imply every visitor is identified or every integration is enabled.

## Audiences

Include Field marketing and CEO alongside marketing leaders, demand generation, campaign teams, operations, and appropriate seller expansion use cases. Match the brief to real evidence about the audience.

Field marketing needs account relevance before, during, and after events. CEOs need a measurable connection between AI adoption, campaign work, and business results. Neither is a guarantee of pipeline.

## Production Checks

- Define AI-driven ABM wherever it appears.
- Keep one-to-one tied to impact or one named-account build.
- Include the signal beat in explanations of how it works.
- Lead with value, then benefits, then product features.
- Source numbers, customer names, quotes, product claims, and logo permissions.
- This messaging update grants no new proof permissions.
- Keep internal agent names and activation layer out of general external copy.
- Do not claim Folloze replaces 6sense or Demandbase.
- Start visual assets with one primary headline. No eyebrow, headline, and explanatory subtitle stack.
- Use 1 through 9 without leading zeros in visual numbering.
- Do not use em dashes.
