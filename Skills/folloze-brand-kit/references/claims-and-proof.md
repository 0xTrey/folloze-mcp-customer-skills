# Folloze Claims And Proof Discipline

Use this public-safe gate before adding any metric, customer name, logo, quote, award, benchmark, competitive claim, product claim, or performance outcome.

## Proof Gate

Every factual claim needs:

1. **Source:** a current public URL or user-approved material.
2. **Fact:** what the source actually supports.
3. **Implication:** why it matters to this audience.
4. **Action:** the next step the fact supports.

No source means no buyer-facing claim. Use qualitative language or an explicit local-review placeholder until the evidence is approved.

## Never Invent

- conversion or engagement lift
- pipeline, bookings, savings, or launch-time numbers
- customer names, logos, quotes, awards, or case studies
- implementation timelines
- product availability, packaging, integrations, or roadmap commitments
- webinar speakers, dates, registration counts, or outcomes

Do not imply that a sourced customer outcome is typical or guaranteed. Choose proof for relevance, not for the largest number.

## Safe Fallbacks

When precise proof is not necessary or unavailable, describe the intended value without a fabricated result: faster campaign creation, more relevant buyer experiences, clearer engagement signal, or more consistent governance.
