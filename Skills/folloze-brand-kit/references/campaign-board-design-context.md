# Folloze Campaign Board Design Context

Use this reference only when Folloze is the visible brand owner. Pair it with `$folloze-board-quality-core` and the route-specific builder.

## Working Context

```text
Campaign design context:
- Brand owner: Folloze
- Audience mood: practical, modern, enterprise-ready, revenue-focused, confident without hype
- Type: clean sans-serif fallback unless a current approved web font is available
- Surfaces: white and pale blue, with selective deep-navy panels
- Buttons: clear filled primary and bordered secondary variants with precise labels
- Modules: crisp borders, restrained shadow, 8px or smaller radius, no cards inside cards
- Media: approved Folloze logo, product visuals, campaign assets, or simple workflow diagrams
- Proof: verified and approved only; qualitative when numeric proof is unavailable
- Motion: restrained, purposeful, and covered by reduced-motion fallbacks
- Interactions: every selector, tab, calculator, resource, or CTA changes state or advances the buyer
- Accessibility: strong contrast, visible focus, mobile line-break checks, no horizontal overflow
- Anti-references: generic SaaS hero, purple-heavy gradient, glassmorphism, fake dashboard chrome, decorative icon tiles, hollow AI hype
- Per-board unknowns: CTA destination, product visuals, offer, and proof permission
```

## Board Registers

- **Demand generation:** offer-led first viewport, clear pain and urgency, relevant resources, concrete campaign CTA.
- **ABM microsite:** buyer-safe account context, specific tension, role relevance, verified proof, workshop or review CTA.
- **Resource hub:** audience promise, featured resource, browsable topic or role paths, tracked resource actions.
- **Product or workbench:** outcome-led first viewport, useful workflow or scenario interaction, implementation path, product-tour CTA.
- **Webinar:** specific event promise, verified speakers and logistics, lifecycle-correct action, supporting resources.
- **Executive briefing:** crisp thesis, operating pressure, personalization/governance/signal value, executive workshop CTA.

## Final Design Gates

- The Folloze logo is readable on the selected surface.
- The first viewport states a specific buyer or campaign outcome.
- `Build. Activate. Signal.` appears only when it advances the story.
- AI is framed as acceleration with review and governance.
- Claims and named proof pass the public-safe proof gate.
- CTA labels are concrete and point to approved destinations.
- No visible copy implies the page was generated, templated, or internally staged.
