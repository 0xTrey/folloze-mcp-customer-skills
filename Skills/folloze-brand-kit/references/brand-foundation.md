# Folloze Brand Foundation

Use this public-safe reference for Folloze positioning, audience, and narrative guardrails.

## External Frame

Folloze helps B2B teams target and convert key accounts. It turns team-created or AI-created content into live, governed, measurable buyer experiences.

Unlike tools that stop at content creation or only identify accounts, Folloze can deploy and host the experience, personalize the path, capture first-party engagement signals, and inform the next marketing or sales move.

Bridge line:

> Folloze is how your content reaches your accounts. And how your accounts reach back.

## Familiar Buyer Language

Prefer:

- ABM and account-based campaign
- campaign destination
- microsite or personalized account experience
- first-party engagement signal
- buyer experience
- campaign and sales follow-up

Do not lead external copy with internal agent names, `activation layer`, `Buyer Experience Platform`, or invented category labels.

## Primary Audiences

- marketing and revenue leaders
- demand generation, ABM, field, campaign, and customer marketers
- marketing and revenue operations
- sales enablement and sales teams when the use case is follow-up or expansion

## Buyer Problems

Connect Folloze to a real problem:

- account programs take too long to launch
- AI can create content, but deployment, governance, personalization, and measurement remain fragmented
- web, design, operations, sales, and agency handoffs slow execution
- personalization does not scale across one-to-one, one-to-few, and one-to-many motions
- generic content destinations fail to reflect the buyer's context
- shallow or disconnected engagement data weakens follow-up
- enterprise brand, security, localization, and approval requirements slow rollout

## Capability Register

| Register | Buyer-facing meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Build | Create and launch personalized campaign destinations and account experiences from approved briefs and content. |
| Activate | Deploy and route governed content across relevant campaign and sales motions. |
| Signal | Capture first-party engagement that helps teams understand interest and plan follow-up. |

Use `Build. Activate. Signal.` for concise display copy when it advances the story. In prose, name the work in plain language.

## Guardrails

- Tie the page to the audience's actual goal or pressure.
- Frame AI as acceleration with human review and governance.
- Use direct, concrete claims that a seller can repeat.
- Do not imply guaranteed performance, full autonomy, AI replacement of marketers, predictive planning, or roadmap capabilities.
