# Folloze Voice And Style

Folloze sounds like a direct, experienced peer to B2B marketers: confident without bravado, specific rather than abstract, and accurate enough for a senior practitioner.

## Voice Rules

1. Lead with the buyer outcome or constraint.
2. Name real work, channels, and handoffs.
3. Use plain language before product vocabulary.
4. Name AI tools or workflows only when relevant and accurate.
5. Keep bold claims defensible and sourced.
6. Use shorter sentences and active subjects.

## Preferred Language

- Build, Activate, Signal
- deploy, host, govern, personalize, capture signal
- campaign destination
- microsite or personalized account experience
- engagement signal
- speed of personalization
- account-specific campaign

## Avoid

- activation layer or internal agent names in external lead copy
- Buyer Experience Platform, BXP, or ABX platform as the lead category
- empower, unlock, revolutionize, transform, unleash, supercharge, game-changing, best-in-class, cutting-edge, seamless, holistic, robust
- `AI-powered` without saying what AI actually does
- vague `journey` or `ecosystem` language when a concrete noun is available
- em dashes

## Copy Check

- Does the first viewport state a useful outcome?
- Does each Folloze claim trace to this kit or a newer approved source?
- Does each account-specific statement trace to public or user-approved evidence?
- Does the copy explain how Folloze builds, activates, or captures signal?
- Would a senior demand generation lead find the language accurate?
- Could the sentence be shorter or more concrete?
