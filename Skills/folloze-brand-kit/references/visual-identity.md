# Folloze Visual Identity

Use this public-safe reference for Folloze-owned digital experiences.

## Logo

Use a current, approved Folloze logo supplied by the user or an authorized public Folloze asset source. This public bundle intentionally does not redistribute logo binaries because the repository does not yet contain explicit brand-asset licensing terms.

- Use the real asset. Do not redraw, stretch, skew, crop, rotate, recolor, outline, shadow, or filter it.
- Keep clear space and verify the actual rendered size.
- Match the approved light or dark variant to the surface and record the source used.

## Direction

Folloze-owned work should feel modern, crisp, enterprise-ready, bright without being playful, easy to scan, and confident without hype.

Prefer:

- large, clear outcome headlines
- mostly white and pale-blue surfaces
- occasional navy system or proof panels
- restrained accent color for state and emphasis
- compact, purposeful modules
- approved product visuals or simple workflow diagrams
- clear filled and bordered CTA variants

Avoid generic purple gradients, decorative blobs, glassmorphism, fake product chrome, icon-tile filler, cards inside cards, oversized empty heroes, and generic stock imagery.
