# Folloze Product Capabilities: Public-Safe

Use this reference only for generic, public-safe capability context. Exact packaging, availability, implementation, usage limits, and commercial terms require a current approved source.

## Public Positioning

Folloze helps B2B teams create personalized buyer experiences, activate those experiences across revenue motions, and capture first-party engagement signals.

## Capability Summary

- **Campaign building:** create governed microsites, campaign destinations, event hubs, content centers, resource pages, and sales rooms.
- **AI-assisted creation:** help teams draft, rewrite, personalize, translate, localize, or generate content, subject to human review and governance.
- **Personalization:** adapt messaging, recommendations, CTAs, and paths using available audience, account, source, behavior, or identity context.
- **Account and contact intelligence:** improve targeting, segmentation, personalization, routing, and follow-up using available data and research.
- **Content organization:** reuse approved assets across experiences and measure content engagement.
- **Integrations:** connect experiences with marketing automation, CRM, ABM, data, sales, and reporting systems where configured.
- **Enterprise scale:** support governed asset use, localization, analytics access, and identity or access controls where enabled.
- **Website engagement:** bring personalized experiences into an existing web journey where configured.
- **Events:** support promotion, engagement, replay, and post-event destinations.
