# Folloze Brand Kit

Use this portable subset as the brand authority for Folloze-owned customer-facing boards. It is derived from the public-safe material in the broader Folloze brand kit and excludes private source pointers, pricing, package rows, customer proof libraries, tracker details, operator identities, and roadmap material.

## Brand Anchor

Folloze helps B2B teams target and convert key accounts.

Your AI creates content. Folloze deploys it, hosts it, governs it, personalizes it, and captures the engagement signals that guide the next move.

Preferred display register:

- Build.
- Activate.
- Signal.

## How To Use

1. Read `references/brand-foundation.md` for positioning, audience, and narrative rules.
2. Read `references/voice-and-style.md` for buyer-facing language.
3. Read `references/visual-identity.md` and obtain a current approved Folloze logo from a verified official or user-authorized source.
4. Use `references/product-capabilities-public-safe.md` only when product capability context is needed.
5. Apply `references/claims-and-proof.md` before any factual, numeric, customer, or competitive claim.
6. Use `references/campaign-board-design-context.md` with `$folloze-board-quality-core` for a Folloze-owned board.

Do not load every reference when the task needs only one or two.

## Boundaries

- Do not add pricing, entitlements, private Drive links, order-form content, contract terms, customer notes, restricted proof, roadmap commitments, local paths, or internal source-system details.
- Do not imply full autonomy, AI replacing marketers, guaranteed results, or unverified capabilities.
- Do not lead external copy with `activation layer`, internal agent names, `Buyer Experience Platform`, or invented category language.
- Newer user-approved Folloze source material may override this subset. Record the conflict and use the newer evidence for that asset.
