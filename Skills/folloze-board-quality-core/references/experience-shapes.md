# Folloze Experience Shapes

Choose one primary page shape before writing HTML or materially restructuring a page.

## Selection Rules

- The approved brand source and current Folloze creation guidance outrank this catalog.
- Choose the shape that fits the buyer job, available proof, and brand evidence.
- Avoid repeating the same hero, three equal cards, final CTA rhythm unless the source brand supports it.
- Do not choose a stat-led or proof-led composition without verified evidence.
- Preserve an approved existing structure unless the user asks for redesign or a clear quality issue requires change.

## Candidate Shapes

- **Workbench:** guided view of the solution in use.
- **Narrative workflow:** numbered stages from current state to improved state.
- **Map or diagram:** a system, flow, ecosystem, or operating model anchors the story.
- **Bento grid:** unequal modules for genuinely varied proof, use cases, resources, and signals.
- **Quote-led or proof-led:** verified customer or third-party evidence leads.
- **Split studio:** alternating text and proof or media panels.
- **Conversational FAQ:** buyer objections and practical answers organize the page.
- **Photographic:** approved source imagery dominates the fold.
- **Long document:** executive memo or dense strategic brief.
- **Ecosystem index:** browsable use-case, industry, persona, content, or resource paths.
